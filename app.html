What we wash

Services

Every job starts with a walkthrough so we can set expectations and describe our restoration process.

House & siding wash

We use a soft washing system from the ground to apply a solution onto the house and rinse off years of green algae, moss, and mold.

Deck washing

We use a professional-grade surface cleaner on all floorboards, railings, and steps of the deck.

Fence washing

Wood and vinyl fencing washed panel by panel so both you and your neighbors get the clean view.

Walkways & patios

Concrete, brick pavers, and stone restored in minutes — moss and grime lifted from every joint.

Driveways & concrete

Even, careful cleaning for driveways, garage pads, and steps so the surface looks uniform again.

Roof wash

We apply a solution onto the roof and post-treat to help ensure organic growth doesn’t return.

Paver restoration

Re-sanding that finishes the job

Pressure washing pavers lifts the dirt — and often the joint sand with it. Cedar Services re-sands walkways and patios after washing so the surface stays locked, level, and ready for traffic again.

  • Pre-treat with solution to kill organic growth
  • Pressure wash the grime off including the joints
  • Spray weed killer to ensure weeds do not grow back
  • Apply polymeric sand in between each paver
  • Finish by applying water to lock and solidify the polymeric sand in place

Cedar Services was founded by WHS students Andrew Easton and Nicolas Demien after noticing an influx of dirty homes across Westwood and neighboring towns. The business has been run for two years and has serviced over 100 homes in the past year alone.

Giving Back

After the first year of pressure washing, we realized we serve in wealthy neighborhoods — yet in some areas of the world, families don’t have the luxury of a roof over their heads.
